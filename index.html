01 About

Senior Site Reliability Engineer with 9+ years of experience designing, automating, and operating scalable, secure, and high-performance cloud platforms. I specialize in building infrastructure that teams can rely on — with a primary focus on AWS, Kubernetes, and modern DevOps practices.

On the infrastructure side, my expertise spans cloud architecture, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), data engineering, and platform observability. I've designed and operated distributed systems that demand high availability, fault tolerance, and low latency at scale.

On the development side, I've worked across the stack with Java, JavaScript, Node.js, Go, Python, and shell scripting — backed by databases and caches including MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and Redis. This full-stack perspective gives me a deeper understanding of the applications running on the infrastructure I build.

Throughout my career I've worked extensively with Kubernetes, Terraform, Docker, AWS Glue, Spark, Delta Lake, Lake Formation, FastAPI, and CI/CD pipelines — building modern data platforms using medallion architecture, developing ETL pipelines, integrating enterprise systems such as NetSuite, and implementing infrastructure as code across cloud environments.

I'm also actively exploring artificial intelligence and generative AI. I've been building Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) solutions using Amazon Bedrock and Claude models, integrating enterprise knowledge bases with conversational AI, and evaluating frameworks such as LangChain and LangGraph for intelligent applications.

Beyond the technical work, I enjoy solving complex infrastructure challenges, improving system reliability, and designing cloud solutions that scale. I have a strong interest in product development and entrepreneurship — particularly SaaS products and AI-powered solutions. Originally from Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India, I'm now based in Guadalajara, Jalisco, Mexico.
